## Not used (deliberately out of scope)

- Ministerial Regulation No. 7 (B.E. 2517) and BMA-BL-2544 ข้อ 84 parking ratios: the tool does not size parking.
- EIA thresholds for condominiums (Ministry of Natural Resources notification): not a geometric rule.
- Condominium Act B.E. 2522: tenure, not massing.
- Draft 4th-revision plan: not in force.
